# Guesstimate 12: How Many Golf Balls / Footballs Fit in a Boeing 747?

## Question

How many golf balls and footballs can fit inside one Boeing 747?

## Clarifying Questions

1. Are we considering one Boeing 747 or multiple aircraft?
2. Should we consider the average size of golf balls and footballs?
3. Should the balls be thrown loosely inside the aircraft or packed properly?
4. Are we considering the entire aircraft or only the usable space?
5. Should we account for the empty space created because spherical balls do not perfectly fill a cube?
6. Should we use cube-shaped boxes to pack the balls?

## Final Question

How many average-sized golf balls and footballs can fit inside one Boeing 747, assuming the balls are packed in average-sized cube boxes and only a certain percentage of the Boeing's internal volume is usable?

## Solution

### Approach

We will estimate the answer separately for golf balls and footballs.

The overall approach is:

**Boeing 747 Internal Volume → Usable Aircraft Volume → Number of Boxes → Ball Volume → Packing Efficiency → Balls per Box → Total Balls**

---

## Assumptions

| Parameter | Golf Balls | Footballs |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average Boeing 747 internal volume | 900 m³ | 900 m³ |
| Usable aircraft volume | 60% | 60% |
| Average ball diameter | 4.3 cm | 22 cm |
| Box dimensions | 50 × 50 × 50 cm | 100 × 100 × 100 cm |
| Packing efficiency inside box | 75% | 75% |

---

# A. Golf Balls

## Step 1: Calculate Usable Boeing 747 Volume

Assume average Boeing 747 internal volume:

**900 m³**

Assume only 60% of the aircraft's internal volume is usable for storing the boxes.

Usable volume:

**900 × 60% = 540 m³**

Convert to cubic centimetres:

**540 × 1,000,000 = 540,000,000 cm³**

Therefore:

**Usable aircraft volume = 540,000,000 cm³**

---

## Step 2: Calculate Volume of One Golf Ball Box

Assume a cube-shaped box of:

**50 cm × 50 cm × 50 cm**

Box volume:

**50 × 50 × 50 = 125,000 cm³**

Therefore:

**One golf ball box = 125,000 cm³**

---

## Step 3: Calculate Number of Golf Ball Boxes

Number of boxes:

**540,000,000 ÷ 125,000 = 4,320 boxes**

Therefore:

**4,320 golf-ball boxes can fit based on volume**

---

## Step 4: Calculate Volume of One Golf Ball

Assume average golf ball diameter:

**4.3 cm**

Radius:

**4.3 ÷ 2 = 2.15 cm**

Golf ball is approximately spherical.

Volume of a sphere:

**V = 4/3 × π × r³**

Therefore:

**V = 4/3 × π × (2.15)³**

**V ≈ 42 cm³**

Therefore:

**Volume of one golf ball ≈ 42 cm³**

---

## Step 5: Calculate Golf Balls per Box

Because golf balls are spherical, they cannot completely fill the cube.

Assume **75% packing efficiency**.

Effective volume available for golf balls:

**125,000 × 75% = 93,750 cm³**

Golf balls per box:

**93,750 ÷ 42 ≈ 2,230 golf balls**

Therefore:

**≈ 2,230 golf balls per box**

---

## Step 6: Calculate Total Golf Balls

Total golf balls:

**Number of boxes × Golf balls per box**

**4,320 × 2,230 = 9,633,600**

Therefore:

**Estimated golf balls ≈ 9.6 million**

---

# B. Footballs

## Step 7: Calculate Usable Boeing 747 Volume

The same usable aircraft volume applies:

**900 × 60% = 540 m³**

Convert to cubic centimetres:

**540 × 1,000,000 = 540,000,000 cm³**

Therefore:

**Usable aircraft volume = 540,000,000 cm³**

---

## Step 8: Calculate Volume of One Football Box

Assume a cube-shaped box of:

**100 cm × 100 cm × 100 cm**

Box volume:

**100 × 100 × 100 = 1,000,000 cm³**

Therefore:

**One football box = 1,000,000 cm³**

---

## Step 9: Calculate Number of Football Boxes

Number of boxes:

**540,000,000 ÷ 1,000,000 = 540 boxes**

Therefore:

**540 football boxes can fit based on volume**

---

## Step 10: Calculate Volume of One Football

A football is not a perfect sphere, but for a guesstimate we approximate its average volume as:

**≈ 5,500 cm³**

Therefore:

**Volume of one football ≈ 5,500 cm³**

---

## Step 11: Calculate Footballs per Box

Assume **75% packing efficiency**.

Effective volume available for footballs:

**1,000,000 × 75% = 750,000 cm³**

Footballs per box:

**750,000 ÷ 5,500 ≈ 136 footballs**

Therefore:

**≈ 136 footballs per box**

---

## Step 12: Calculate Total Footballs

Total footballs:

**Number of boxes × Footballs per box**

**540 × 136 = 73,440**

Therefore:

**Estimated footballs ≈ 73,000**

---

# Final Estimate

| Ball Type | Estimated Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Golf Balls | **≈ 9.6 million** |
| Footballs | **≈ 73,000** |
