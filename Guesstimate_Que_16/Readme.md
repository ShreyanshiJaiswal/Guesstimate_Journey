# Guesstimate 16 — Smartphones Sold Per Year in India

## Clarifying Questions

- Is the estimate required for a day, week, month, or year?
- Should we estimate for a city, district, state, or the entire country?
- Should all smartphone models and connectivity types such as 4G and 5G be considered?
- Should second-hand smartphones be included?
- Should the smartphone replacement cycle be considered?
- Should both rural and urban India be considered?
- Should people owning more than one smartphone be considered?

## Final Question

Estimate the number of smartphones sold per year across India, considering both rural and urban populations, all smartphone models, smartphone ownership across households, multiple smartphones per household, and the average smartphone replacement cycle.

## Solution

### Key Logic

**Population → Urban/Rural → Income → Households → Smartphones per Household → Total Smartphone Base → Replacement Cycle → Annual Smartphone Sales**

### Step 1: Total Population

Assume India's population is approximately **1.4 billion**.

- Urban: 40% = **560 million**
- Rural: 60% = **840 million**

---

### Step 2: Urban Population

Divide the urban population into income segments:

| Income Segment | Share | Population |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rich | 10% | 56M |
| Middle | 60% | 336M |
| Lower | 30% | 168M |
| **Total** | **100%** | **560M** |

Assume average household sizes:

- Rich: **3 people/household**
- Middle: **4 people/household**
- Lower: **5 people/household**

Therefore:

- Rich households = 56M ÷ 3 ≈ **18.7M**
- Middle households = 336M ÷ 4 = **84M**
- Lower households = 168M ÷ 5 ≈ **33.6M**

---

### Step 3: Urban Smartphone Ownership

Assume:

- Rich households own an average of **5 smartphones**
- Middle-income households own an average of **4 smartphones**
- Lower-income households own an average of **3 smartphones**

This accounts for differences in smartphone ownership and people owning more than one smartphone.

#### Urban Rich

18.7M households × 5 smartphones

= **93.5M smartphones**

#### Urban Middle

84M households × 4 smartphones

= **336M smartphones**

#### Urban Lower

33.6M households × 3 smartphones

= **100.8M smartphones**

### Total Urban Smartphones

93.5M + 336M + 100.8M

= **530.3M smartphones**

---

### Step 4: Rural Population

Divide the rural population into income segments:

| Income Segment | Share | Population |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rich | 5% | 42M |
| Middle | 35% | 294M |
| Lower | 60% | 504M |
| **Total** | **100%** | **840M** |

Assume average household sizes:

- Rich: **4 people/household**
- Middle: **6 people/household**
- Lower: **8 people/household**

Therefore:

- Rich households = 42M ÷ 4 = **10.5M**
- Middle households = 294M ÷ 6 = **49M**
- Lower households = 504M ÷ 8 = **63M**

---

### Step 5: Rural Smartphone Ownership

Assume:

- Rich rural households: **1 smartphone/household**
- Middle rural households: **2 smartphones/household**
- Lower rural households: **1 smartphone/household**

This reflects lower smartphone ownership in rural and lower-income households.

#### Rural Rich

10.5M households × 1 smartphone

= **10.5M smartphones**

#### Rural Middle

49M households × 2 smartphones

= **98M smartphones**

#### Rural Lower

63M households × 1 smartphone

= **63M smartphones**

### Total Rural Smartphones

10.5M + 98M + 63M

= **171.5M smartphones**

---

### Step 6: Total Smartphones in Use

Urban smartphones = **530.3M**

Rural smartphones = **171.5M**

Therefore:

530.3M + 171.5M

= **701.8M smartphones**

≈ **700M smartphones**

---

### Step 7: Annual Smartphone Sales

Assume the average smartphone has a **5-year replacement cycle**.

Therefore:

700M ÷ 5

= **140M smartphones/year**

## Final Estimate

**≈ 140 million smartphones are sold per year in India.**
