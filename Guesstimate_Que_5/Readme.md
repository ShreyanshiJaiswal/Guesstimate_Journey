# Guesstimate 5: Office Chairs Sold in India

## Clarifying Questions

1. Are we considering the entire country?
   - **Assumption:** Yes, all states and regions of India are covered.

2. Are we estimating sales for a specific month or for the entire year?
   - **Assumption:** We are estimating **annual office chair sales**.

---

## Final Question

**Estimate the total number of office chairs sold in India in one year.**

---

## Solution

We will estimate the existing number of people who use office chairs and then calculate how many chairs need to be replaced every year, along with additional chairs required due to growth in office-based employment.

### Key Logic

**Total Office Chairs Sold Annually = Replacement Demand + New Demand**

Where:

- **Replacement Demand** = Existing office-chair base ÷ Average chair lifespan
- **New Demand** = Existing office-chair base × Annual growth rate

---

## Step 1: Estimate India's Urban and Rural Population

Assume India's total population = **1.4 billion**

### Urban Population

Urban population = 40%

**Calculation:**

1.4 billion × 40% = **560 million**

### Rural Population

Rural population = 60%

**Calculation:**

1.4 billion × 60% = **840 million**

Therefore:

- **Urban population = 560 million**
- **Rural population = 840 million**

---

## Step 2: Estimate Employed Population

### Urban Employed Population

Assume 80% of the urban population is employed.

**Calculation:**

560 million × 80% = **448 million**

### Rural Employed Population

Assume 50% of the rural population is employed.

**Calculation:**

840 million × 50% = **420 million**

Therefore:

- **Urban employed population = 448 million**
- **Rural employed population = 420 million**

---

## Step 3: Estimate People Who Use Office Chairs

Not every employed person works in an office. Therefore, we estimate the percentage of employed people who work in office-based environments.

### Urban Office-Chair Users

Assume 60% of employed urban people work in offices.

**Calculation:**

448 million × 60% = **268.8 million**

≈ **270 million**

### Rural Office-Chair Users

Assume 20% of employed rural people work in offices.

**Calculation:**

420 million × 20% = **84 million**

### Total Office-Chair User Base

**Calculation:**

270 million + 84 million = **354 million**

≈ **350 million**

**Existing office-chair user base = 350 million**

---

## Step 4: Estimate Replacement Demand

Assume the average lifespan of an office chair is **8 years**.

Therefore, approximately 1/8th of the existing office-chair base needs to be replaced every year.

**Calculation:**

350 million ÷ 8 = **43.75 million**

≈ **44 million**

**Annual replacement demand = 44 million chairs**

---

## Step 5: Estimate New Demand from Growth

Additional chairs are required because of:

- New businesses
- Office expansion
- New employees
- New branches and offices

Assume annual growth in the office-chair base = **5%**

**Calculation:**

350 million × 5% = **17.5 million**

≈ **18 million**

**New annual demand = 18 million chairs**

---

## Step 6: Calculate Total Annual Office Chair Sales

**Total Annual Sales = Replacement Demand + New Demand**

Using the rounded figures:

44 million + 18 million = **62 million**

Using the unrounded figures:

43.75 million + 17.5 million = **61.25 million**

Therefore:

**Estimated annual office chair sales ≈ 60 million chairs**

---

## Summary

| Component | Calculation | Estimate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total population | Assumption | 1.4B |
| Urban population | 1.4B × 40% | 560M |
| Rural population | 1.4B × 60% | 840M |
| Urban employed population | 560M × 80% | 448M |
| Rural employed population | 840M × 50% | 420M |
| Urban office-chair users | 448M × 60% | 270M |
| Rural office-chair users | 420M × 20% | 84M |
| Existing office-chair user base | 270M + 84M | **350M** |
| Replacement demand | 350M ÷ 8 | **44M/year** |
| New demand from growth | 350M × 5% | **18M/year** |
| **Total annual office chair sales** | 44M + 18M | **≈ 60M/year** |

---

## Key Assumptions

- India's population = **1.4 billion**
- Urban population = **40%**
- Rural population = **60%**
- Urban employment rate = **80%**
- Rural employment rate = **50%**
- Urban office-chair usage among employed population = **60%**
- Rural office-chair usage among employed population = **20%**
- Average office-chair lifespan = **8 years**
- Annual growth in office-chair demand = **5%**
- Estimate covers **all of India**
- Estimate represents **annual office chair sales**

---

## Final Estimate

> **India sells approximately 60 million office chairs per year.**
