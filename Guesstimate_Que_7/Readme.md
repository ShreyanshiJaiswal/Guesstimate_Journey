# Guesstimate 7: Distance Between New York and Delhi

## Clarifying Questions

1. Are we estimating the **shortest straight-line distance** between New York and Delhi?
2. Should we consider the **great-circle route**, i.e., the shortest path between two points on Earth?
3. We do not need to estimate an actual flight route, since flight paths depend on airlines, airspace restrictions, and weather.

## Final Question

**Estimate the shortest straight-line distance between New York and New Delhi.**

## Solution

We can estimate the distance by dividing the journey into major geographical checkpoints rather than calculating the distance country by country.

### Key Logic

Since New York and Delhi are on opposite sides of the Atlantic and Eurasian landmass, the shortest route broadly travels eastward from New York, across the Atlantic toward Europe, through West Asia, and finally into India.

We estimate the distance of each major section and add them together.

A simple checkpoint-based breakdown is:

**New York → Europe → West Asia → Delhi**

### Step-by-Step Calculation

| Segment | Estimated Distance |
|---|---:|
| New York → Europe | ~5,500 km |
| Europe → West Asia | ~2,500 km |
| West Asia → Delhi | ~4,000 km |
| **Total** | **~12,000 km** |

Therefore:

**5,500 + 2,500 + 4,000 = 12,000 km**

### Final Estimate

**Distance between New York and New Delhi ≈ 12,000 km**
