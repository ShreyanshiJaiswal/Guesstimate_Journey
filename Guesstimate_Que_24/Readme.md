# Guesstimate 24: Diaper Market in India

## Clarifying Questions

- Adult diapers or infant diapers?

- Should competitors/other brands be included?

## Final Question

What is the annual infant diaper market in India across all brands?

> **Assumption:** Since no time period is specified, the market is estimated on an annual basis.

---

## Solution

### 1. Total Infant Population

- India's population ≈ **1.4 billion**
- Children aged 0–4 years ≈ **8.3%** of the population.

**1.4B × 8.3% ≈ 116M**

Round to **115M children aged 0–4 years**.

---

### 2. Age-wise Population Split

| Age Group | Share | Children |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0–6 months | 12% | 13.8M |
| 6–12 months | 12% | 13.8M |
| 1–2 years | 25% | 28.8M |
| 2–3 years | 25% | 28.8M |
| 3–4 years | 26% | 29.9M |
| **Total** | **100%** | **115M** |

---

### 3. Diaper Usage by Age

Diaper usage is assumed to decline with age as children gradually become less dependent on diapers and begin toilet training.

| Age Group | Diapers/Day |
|---|---:|
| 0–6 months | 5 |
| 6–12 months | 4 |
| 1–2 years | 3 |
| 2–3 years | 2 |
| 3–4 years | 1 |

---

### 4. Theoretical Annual Diaper Demand

**0–6 months**

13.8M × 5 × 365 = **25.2B diapers**

**6–12 months**

13.8M × 4 × 365 = **20.1B diapers**

**1–2 years**

28.8M × 3 × 365 = **31.5B diapers**

**2–3 years**

28.8M × 2 × 365 = **21.0B diapers**

**3–4 years**

29.9M × 1 × 365 = **10.9B diapers**

**Total theoretical demand = 108.7B diapers/year**

---

### 5. Rural vs. Urban Usage

The 115M children are split into:

- **Urban: 40% = 46M**
- **Rural: 60% = 69M**

Assume:

- Urban diaper usage = **70%**
- Rural diaper usage = **30%**

Urban usage is higher due to greater affordability, awareness and availability, while rural usage is lower due to affordability and accessibility constraints.

Since the two groups have different population sizes, a **weighted average** is used to derive one overall usage rate. This gives greater importance to the larger rural population.

**Urban contribution**

40% × 70% = **28%**

**Rural contribution**

60% × 30% = **18%**

**Overall effective usage**

28% + 18% = **46%**

Therefore:

108.7B × 46% ≈ **50B diapers/year**

---

### 6. Average Price per Diaper

Assume an average realized price of **₹10 per diaper**, accounting for different sizes, brands, pack sizes and value/premium products.

---

### 7. Market Size

50B × ₹10 = **₹500B**

₹500B = **₹50,000 crore**

---

## Final Estimate

**₹50,000 crore/year**
