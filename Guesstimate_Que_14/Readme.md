# Guesstimate 14: Passengers Traveling in Delhi Metro

## Question

Estimate the number of passengers traveling through the Delhi Metro in one day.

## Clarifying Questions

1. Are we estimating daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly passengers?
2. Are we considering the entire day or a particular time period?
3. Are we considering only Delhi or the entire Delhi NCR region?
4. Are tourists included?
5. Are we estimating unique passengers or total passenger journeys?

## Final Question

**How many passenger journeys take place through the Delhi Metro in one day, across the entire day, covering Delhi and including tourists?**

---

# Solution

## Step 1: Estimate the Total Population

Assume:

- Delhi population = **22M**
- Tourist visits per year ≈ **36–40M**
- Average daily tourist visits = 36–40M ÷ 365 ≈ **0.1M/day**

Therefore:

**Total population base = 22M + 0.1M = 22.1M**

---

## Step 2: Divide Delhi Population by Income Group

| Income Group | Share | Population |
|---|---:|---:|
| Upper | 10% | 2.2M |
| Middle | 50% | 11.0M |
| Lower | 40% | 8.8M |
| **Total** | **100%** | **22.0M** |

---

## Step 3: Divide Each Group into Daily Commuters and Non-Commuters

Assumptions:

- Upper-income → 5% daily commuters
- Middle-income → 70% daily commuters
- Lower-income → 60% daily commuters

### Upper Income

Daily commuters:

**2.2M × 5% = 0.11M**

Non-commuters:

**2.2M - 0.11M = 2.09M**

### Middle Income

Daily commuters:

**11M × 70% = 7.7M**

Non-commuters:

**11M - 7.7M = 3.3M**

### Lower Income

Daily commuters:

**8.8M × 60% = 5.28M**

Non-commuters:

**8.8M - 5.28M = 3.52M**

| Income Group | Daily Commuters | Non-Commuters |
|---|---:|---:|
| Upper | 0.11M | 2.09M |
| Middle | 7.70M | 3.30M |
| Lower | 5.28M | 3.52M |
| **Total** | **13.09M** | **8.91M** |

---

## Step 4: Estimate Metro Usage Among Daily Commuters

Not every commuter uses the Metro because some use cars, buses, bikes, autos, or cabs.

Assume Metro adoption is higher among middle- and lower-income commuters because Metro is a relatively affordable and reliable mode of daily transportation.

- Upper-income commuters → **15% use Metro**
- Middle-income commuters → **45% use Metro**
- Lower-income commuters → **60% use Metro**

### Upper-Income Metro Commuters

**0.11M × 15% = 0.02M**

### Middle-Income Metro Commuters

**7.7M × 45% = 3.47M**

### Lower-Income Metro Commuters

**5.28M × 60% = 3.17M**

Therefore:

**Total Metro commuters = 0.02 + 3.47 + 3.17**

**= 6.66M people**

---

## Step 5: Estimate Metro Usage Among Non-Commuters

Non-commuters may use the Metro for shopping, education, entertainment, medical visits, social visits, etc.

Assume **15% of non-commuters use Metro on a given day**.

**8.91M × 15% = 1.34M Metro users**

---

## Step 6: Estimate Metro Usage Among Tourists

Assume **60% of tourists use the Metro** because it is a convenient and widely connected mode of transport within Delhi.

**0.1M × 60% = 0.06M Metro users**

---

## Step 7: Calculate Unique Metro Users

**Metro commuters = 6.66M**

**Metro non-commuters = 1.34M**

**Metro tourists = 0.06M**

Therefore:

**Total unique Metro users = 6.66 + 1.34 + 0.06**

**= 8.06M people/day**

---

## Step 8: Convert Users into Passenger Journeys

We are estimating **passenger journeys**, so one person can make multiple Metro trips in a day.

### Commuters

Assume 2 journeys per day:

**6.66M × 2 = 13.32M journeys**

### Non-Commuters

Assume 1.5 journeys per day:

**1.34M × 1.5 = 2.01M journeys**

### Tourists

Assume 1.5 journeys per day:

**0.06M × 1.5 = 0.09M journeys**

---

# Step 9: Final Estimate

**Total daily passenger journeys**

= 13.32M + 2.01M + 0.09M

= **15.42M**

### Final Answer

**≈ 15 million Delhi Metro passenger journeys per day**
