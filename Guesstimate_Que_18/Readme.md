# Guesstimate 18: Annual Household & Transportation Natural Gas Demand in India

## Clarifying Questions

1. Are we estimating demand for a day, month, or year?
2. Are we estimating demand for the whole of India or a specific city/state?
3. Which sectors should be considered?
4. Should household consumption be considered through PNG?
5. Should transportation consumption be considered through CNG?
6. Should industrial consumption be included?

## Final Question

Estimate the annual demand for natural gas used by households and transportation in India, considering PNG and CNG consumption.

---

## Solution

### Key Logic

Natural gas demand is estimated separately for:

- Household → PNG
- Transportation → CNG

Industrial consumption is excluded to keep the guesstimate focused and interview-friendly, as industrial demand would require a separate estimation of large consumers such as fertilizer plants, refineries, petrochemical units and other industries.

### Framework

India's natural gas demand
→ Household demand (PNG) + Transportation demand (CNG)

---

## 1. Household Demand — PNG

### Step 1: Estimate India's Population

Assume India's population = **1.4 billion**

Split the population into urban and rural:

- Urban = 40%
- Rural = 60%

#### Urban Population

= 1.4B × 40%

= **560M**

#### Rural Population

= 1.4B × 60%

= **840M**

The 40:60 split provides a simple approximation of India's urban-rural population distribution.

---

### Step 2: Estimate Number of Households

Assume:

- Urban household size = **4 people**
- Rural household size = **5 people**

Urban households are assumed to be smaller, while rural households are assumed to have slightly more people per household.

#### Urban Households

= 560M ÷ 4

= **140M households**

#### Rural Households

= 840M ÷ 5

= **168M households**

#### Total Households

= 140M + 168M

= **308M households**

---

### Step 3: Estimate PNG-Using Households

PNG is primarily available in urban areas and locations covered by City Gas Distribution networks. Therefore, PNG penetration is assumed to be higher in urban areas than rural areas.

Assume:

- Urban PNG penetration = **10%**
- Rural PNG penetration = **2%**

#### Urban PNG Households

= 140M × 10%

= **14M households**

#### Rural PNG Households

= 168M × 2%

= **3.36M households**

#### Total PNG-Using Households

= 14M + 3.36M

= **17.36M households**

---

### Step 4: Estimate Monthly PNG Consumption

Assume average PNG consumption per household = **30 SCM/month**.

This is a rounded estimate for normal domestic PNG usage, primarily for cooking and other household requirements.

#### Monthly PNG Demand

= 17.36M × 30 SCM

= **520.8M SCM/month**

= **0.521 BCM/month**

---

### Step 5: Estimate Annual PNG Demand

#### Annual PNG Demand

= 0.521 BCM × 12

= **6.25 BCM/year**

Therefore:

**Annual Household PNG Demand ≈ 6.3 BCM**

---

## 2. Transportation Demand — CNG

For transportation, a vehicle-based approach is used because CNG demand depends on the number of CNG vehicles and their usage rather than directly on population.

### Step 1: Estimate CNG Vehicles

Assume:

**CNG vehicles = 8M**

This includes cars, taxis, auto-rickshaws, buses and other CNG vehicles.

---

### Step 2: Estimate Average CNG Consumption

Different types of CNG vehicles have different fuel consumption, so a blended average is used.

Assume:

**Average CNG consumption = 5 kg/vehicle/day**

Use the approximate conversion:

**1 kg CNG ≈ 1.38 SCM**

Therefore:

#### Daily CNG Consumption per Vehicle

= 5 × 1.38

= **6.9 SCM/day**

---

### Step 3: Estimate Annual CNG Demand

#### Annual CNG Demand

= CNG Vehicles × Daily Consumption × 365

= 8M × 6.9 × 365

= **20,148M SCM/year**

= **20.15 BCM/year**

Therefore:

**Annual Transportation CNG Demand ≈ 20.1 BCM**

---

## 3. Total Annual Natural Gas Demand

### Household PNG Demand

= **6.25 BCM/year**

### Transportation CNG Demand

= **20.15 BCM/year**

### Total Demand

= 6.25 + 20.15

= **26.40 BCM/year**

---

## Final Estimate

**Annual Household + Transportation Natural Gas Demand in India ≈ 26 BCM/year**

For an interview, this can be rounded to:

**≈ 25–30 BCM/year**

---

## Summary

| Sector | Annual Demand |
|---|---:|
| Household — PNG | **6.25 BCM** |
| Transportation — CNG | **20.15 BCM** |
| **Total** | **26.40 BCM/year** |

---

## Key Assumptions

| Assumption | Value | Reason |
|---|---:|---|
| India population | 1.4B | Rounded population estimate |
| Urban population | 40% | Simplified urban-rural split |
| Rural population | 60% | Simplified urban-rural split |
| Urban household size | 4 | Urban households assumed to be smaller |
| Rural household size | 5 | Rural households assumed to be slightly larger |
| Urban PNG penetration | 10% | PNG is concentrated in urban and CGD-covered areas |
| Rural PNG penetration | 2% | PNG availability is lower in rural areas |
| PNG consumption | 30 SCM/household/month | Rounded average domestic consumption |
| CNG vehicles | 8M | Rounded estimate across major CNG vehicle categories |
| CNG consumption | 5 kg/vehicle/day | Blended average across different vehicle types |

| Industrial consumption | Excluded | Keeps the guesstimate focused and interview-friendly |

---

## Final Answer

**India's annual household and transportation demand for natural gas is estimated at approximately 26 BCM, or roughly 25–30 BCM per year.**
