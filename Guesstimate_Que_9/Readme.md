# Guesstimate 9: Number of T-Shirts Sold by E-commerce Companies Per Day


## Clarifying Questions

1. Are we considering a particular region/country or the entire globe?
2. Are we considering every e-commerce company or only major e-commerce platforms?
3. Are the T-shirts purchased for personal use or commercial purposes?
4. Are we considering a specific time frame or a normal day?
5. Are second-hand T-shirts included?
6. Are luxury brands included, or only normal/regular brands?

---

## Final Question

**How many normal-brand T-shirts are sold through e-commerce platforms in India on a normal day for personal use, including second-hand T-shirts?**

---

## Solution

### Key Logic

We will estimate annual T-shirt purchases through e-commerce by:

1. Dividing India's population into rich, middle, and poor classes.
2. Estimating the percentage of each group that uses e-commerce.
3. Further dividing the middle class into upper-middle, middle-middle, and lower-middle classes.
4. Estimating the number of T-shirts purchased per e-commerce user per year.
5. Converting the annual estimate into daily sales by dividing by 365.

---

## 1. India's Population

Assume India's population ≈ **1.4 billion**

| Income Group | Population Share | Population |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rich / Upper Class | 10% | 0.14 billion |
| Middle Class | 30% | 0.42 billion |
| Poor / Lower Class | 60% | 0.84 billion |
| **Total** | **100%** | **1.40 billion** |

---

## 2. Rich / Upper Class

Rich / Upper Class population = **0.14 billion**

We assume only **2%** of this group purchases T-shirts through e-commerce.

We assume **2 T-shirts per user per year**.

| Metric | Assumption / Calculation |
|---|---:|
| Population | 0.14 billion |
| E-commerce users | 2% |
| E-commerce users | 0.14 × 2% = 0.0028 billion |
| T-shirts per user/year | 2 |
| Annual T-shirts | 0.0028 × 2 = 0.0056 billion |
| Annual T-shirts | **5.6 million** |

The assumption of **2 T-shirts per user per year** is relatively high because the rich/upper-class group has greater purchasing power and can afford premium or luxury clothing.

---

## 3. Middle Class

Middle-class population = **0.42 billion**

We divide the middle class into three segments:

| Middle-Class Segment | Share of Middle Class | Population | T-Shirts/User/Year |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Upper Middle | 60% | 0.252 billion | 5 |
| Middle Middle | 30% | 0.126 billion | 2 |
| Lower Middle | 10% | 0.042 billion | 1 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **0.420 billion** | — |

### Upper Middle

`0.42 × 60% = 0.252 billion users`

`0.252 × 5 = 1.26 billion T-shirts/year`

= **1,260 million T-shirts/year**

### Middle Middle

`0.42 × 30% = 0.126 billion users`

`0.126 × 2 = 0.252 billion T-shirts/year`

= **252 million T-shirts/year**

### Lower Middle

`0.42 × 10% = 0.042 billion users`

`0.042 × 1 = 0.042 billion T-shirts/year`

= **42 million T-shirts/year**

---

## 4. Poor / Lower Class

Poor / Lower Class population = **0.84 billion**

We assume **10%** of this group uses e-commerce.

| Metric | Assumption / Calculation |
|---|---:|
| Population | 0.84 billion |
| E-commerce users | 10% |
| Potential e-commerce users | 0.84 × 10% = 0.084 billion |
| T-shirts considered | 0 |

For this estimate, we assume **0 T-shirts** from this segment because we are focusing on normal-brand e-commerce purchases and are not considering this group as a meaningful contributor to the estimated e-commerce T-shirt demand.

---

## 5. Total Annual T-Shirt Sales

| Segment | Annual T-Shirt Sales |
|---|---:|
| Rich / Upper Class | 5.6 million |
| Upper Middle | 1,260 million |
| Middle Middle | 252 million |
| Lower Middle | 42 million |
| Poor / Lower Class | 0 |
| **Total** | **1,559.6 million** |

Therefore:

**Annual T-shirt sales ≈ 1,560 million**

For easier calculation:

**≈ 1,500 million T-shirts/year**

---

## 6. Daily T-Shirt Sales

Assuming sales are distributed across 365 days:

`1,500 million ÷ 365`

`≈ 4.1 million`

Therefore:

## Final Estimate

**≈ 4 million T-shirts are sold through e-commerce platforms in India per day on a normal day.**

### Answer: **~4 million T-shirts/day**
