## Final Question

Assuming we are considering the **entire Delhi region**, personal-use cars, including the existing car population, how many **red-colored Maruti Suzuki Swift cars** could be present in Delhi?

## Solution

### Key Logic

We will estimate the number of red-colored Swift cars using the following framework:

**Delhi Population → Income Segments → Car Ownership → Total Cars → Swift Cars → Red Swift Cars**

---

## Step 1: Delhi Population

Assume Delhi has a population of approximately **20 million people**.

We divide the population into three broad income segments:

| Income Segment | % of Population | Population |
|---|---:|---:|
| Upper / Rich | 20% | 4 million |
| Middle | 40% | 8 million |
| Poor | 40% | 8 million |
| **Total** | **100%** | **20 million** |

### Assumption

- **Upper/Rich:** Not considered further because they can afford premium or luxury cars and are assumed to be less likely to choose a Swift.
- **Middle:** Considered further because Swift is an affordable and practical car for this segment.
- **Poor:** Not considered further because they are assumed to be unable to afford a car.

Therefore:

**Population considered = 40% × 20 million = 8 million**

---

## Step 2: Divide the Middle-Income Population

The 8 million middle-income population is divided into three groups:

| Middle-Income Segment | % of Middle Class | Population |
|---|---:|---:|
| Upper Middle | 10% | 0.8 million |
| Middle Middle | 30% | 2.4 million |
| Lower Middle | 60% | 4.8 million |
| **Total** | **100%** | **8 million** |

---

## Step 3: Estimate Number of Cars

Assume:

- **Upper Middle:** Can afford an average of **2 cars**
- **Middle Middle:** Can afford an average of **1 car**
- **Lower Middle:** Assumed to own **0 cars**

### Upper Middle

**0.8 million × 2 = 1.6 million cars**

### Middle Middle

**2.4 million × 1 = 2.4 million cars**

### Lower Middle

**4.8 million × 0 = 0 cars**

Therefore:

**Total cars = 1.6 million + 2.4 million + 0**

**Total cars = 4 million**

---

## Step 4: Estimate Swift Cars

Assume **60% of these cars are Maruti Suzuki Swift cars**, because Swift is an affordable and popular car among middle-income consumers.

**Swift cars = 4 million × 60%**

**Swift cars = 2.4 million**

---

## Step 5: Estimate Red Swift Cars

Assume **20% of Swift cars are red-colored**.

### Why 20%?

- Cars are more commonly purchased in **neutral colors such as black, white, and grey** rather than vibrant colors such as red.
- Since **men tend to form a larger share of car buyers**, and the assumption is that men are generally less inclined toward vibrant colors such as red, the share of red cars is assumed to be lower.
- Therefore, only **20% of Swift cars are assumed to be red-colored**.

**Red Swift cars = 2.4 million × 20%**

**Red Swift cars = 0.48 million**

---

# Final Estimate

**0.48 million = 480,000**

Therefore, the estimated number of **red-colored Swift cars in Delhi is approximately:**

## **4.8 lakh red Swift cars**
