# Guesstimate 10: How Many Cows Are There in India?

## Clarifying Questions

1. Are we considering the entire country or a particular state?
2. Are all breeds of cows included?
3. Are freely moving/stray cows included?
4. Are cows kept in houses and dairies for milk production included?
5. Are calves included?
6. Are both rural and urban areas included?
7. Are we estimating the current number or a historical/future number?
8. Are imported cows included?
9. Should mortality rate be considered?

---

## Final Question

**How many cows are currently present across India, including all breeds, freely moving cows and cows kept in houses/dairies, across both rural and urban areas, while excluding imported cows?**

---

# Solution

## Key Logic

We estimate the cow population using households as the primary unit.

**India's Population**
→ **Rural / Urban Population**
→ **Number of Households**
→ **Households Owning Cows**
→ **Average Cows per Cow-owning Household**
→ **Household/Dairy Cows**

Freely moving/stray cows are estimated separately and then added to the household/dairy population.

---

## Step 1: India's Population

Assume India's population = **1.4 billion**

| Segment | Population Share | Population |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rural | 60% | 840 million |
| Urban | 40% | 560 million |
| **Total** | **100%** | **1.4 billion** |

---

## Step 2: Estimate Number of Households

Assume average household size = **4 people**

### Rural Households

\[
840M \div 4 = 210M
\]

**Rural households ≈ 210 million**

### Urban Households

\[
560M \div 4 = 140M
\]

**Urban households ≈ 140 million**

---

## Step 3: Estimate Cow-owning Households

Assume:

- **20% of rural households** own/keep cows
- **5% of urban households** own/keep cows

### Rural Cow-owning Households

\[
210M \times 20\% = 42M
\]

**≈ 42 million households**

### Urban Cow-owning Households

\[
140M \times 5\% = 7M
\]

**≈ 7 million households**

---

## Step 4: Estimate Cows per Cow-owning Household

Assume:

- Rural = **3 cows per household**
- Urban = **2 cows per household**

### Rural Cows

\[
42M \times 3 = 126M
\]

**≈ 126 million cows**

### Urban Cows

\[
7M \times 2 = 14M
\]

**≈ 14 million cows**

### Total Household/Dairy Cows

\[
126M + 14M = 140M
\]

**≈ 140 million cows**

---

## Step 5: Estimate Freely Moving/Stray Cows

Assume freely moving/stray cows = **10 million**

---

## Step 6: Final Estimate

| Category | Estimated Cows |
|---|---:|
| Rural household/dairy cows | 126 million |
| Urban household/dairy cows | 14 million |
| Freely moving/stray cows | 10 million |
| **Total** | **150 million** |

Therefore:

\[
140M + 10M = 150M
\]

# Final Estimate

**≈ 150 million cows in India**
