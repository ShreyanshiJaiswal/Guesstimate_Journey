# Guesstimate 20: Number of People at IGI Airport Daily

## Final Question

Estimate the total number of people present at IGI Airport throughout the day, including domestic and international passengers, both arrivals and departures, crew members, airport staff and management, and visitors such as family members, friends, and cab drivers.

## Solution

### 1. Flights Taking Off from IGI Airport

Reference: **Guesstimate 13 – Number of Flights Taking Off in India in a Day**

From Guesstimate 13, the estimated number of flights taking off across India was approximately **7,000 per day**.

IGI Airport is one of India's largest and busiest airports, so assume it accounts for approximately **15%** of India's daily flight departures.

**7,000 × 15% = 1,050 ≈ 1,000 departures/day**

For ease of calculation, we take:

**Flights taking off from IGI = 1,000/day**

Every departing flight has a corresponding arrival, so:

**Flights arriving = 1,000/day**

Therefore:

**Total flights handled = 2,000/day**

---

### 2. Domestic Passenger Footfall

Assume the flight mix is approximately:

- **70% domestic**
- **30% international**

This gives a reasonable split because domestic air travel represents the majority of flight traffic in India.

Domestic departures:

**1,000 × 70% = 700 flights**

Assume an average of **180 passengers per domestic flight**.

This represents a reasonable average across different aircraft sizes and load factors.

Domestic departing passengers:

**700 × 180 = 126,000**

Since the same number of passengers arrive through the corresponding incoming flights:

Domestic arriving passengers:

**126,000**

Therefore:

**Total domestic passenger footfall = 126,000 × 2 = 252,000**

---

### 3. International Passenger Footfall

International departures:

**1,000 × 30% = 300 flights**

Assume an average of **250 passengers per international flight** because international flights generally use larger aircraft and carry more passengers.

International departing passengers:

**300 × 250 = 75,000**

International arriving passengers:

**75,000**

Therefore:

**Total international passenger footfall = 75,000 × 2 = 150,000**

---

### 4. Total Passenger Footfall

**Domestic + International**

**252,000 + 150,000 = 402,000 passenger movements/day**

---

### 5. Crew Members

Assume **8 crew members per flight**:

- 2 pilots
- 6 cabin crew

For 1,000 departing flights:

**1,000 × 8 = 8,000 crew members**

The crew is counted once because the same crew members operate the corresponding arrival/departure movement rather than representing completely different people.

---

### 6. Airport Staff and Management

Assume approximately **7,000 airport personnel** across security, baggage handling, cleaning, airport operations, airline services, retail, restaurants, ground handling, and management.

**Staff and management = 7,000**

---

### 7. Visitors

Assume visitors are approximately **30% of total passenger movements**.

This accounts for people accompanying passengers, receiving arriving passengers, family members, friends, and other airport visitors.

**402,000 × 30% = 120,600 visitors**

---

### 8. Total Daily Footfall

| Category | People/day |
|---|---:|
| Domestic passengers | 252,000 |
| International passengers | 150,000 |
| Crew | 8,000 |
| Airport staff & management | 7,000 |
| Visitors | 120,600 |
| **Total** | **537,600** |

## Final Estimate

**Estimated daily footfall at IGI Airport ≈ 5.4 lakh people per day.**

### Key Logic

**Daily Footfall = Passengers + Crew + Airport Staff + Visitors**

**= 402,000 + 8,000 + 7,000 + 120,600**

**= 537,600**

**Final Estimate ≈ 5.4 lakh people/day**
