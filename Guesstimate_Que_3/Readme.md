# Guesstimate: Domestic Refrigerator Sales in India

## Business Question

**Estimate the domestic refrigerator sales in India.**

---

## Clarifying Questions

Before estimating, we clarify the following:

- Do we need to estimate refrigerator sales monthly or annually?
- Should second-hand/resale refrigerators be included?
- Are we considering refrigerators for domestic/residential use only, or should commercial refrigerators also be included?
- Should we consider all types of refrigerators, such as standard and smart refrigerators?

---

## Final Clarified Question

**Estimate the number of new domestic/residential refrigerators sold in India in one year, excluding second-hand and commercial refrigerators.**

---

## Framework

**Total Population**
→ **Urban Population**
→ **Urban Households**
→ **Income Segmentation**
→ **Upper + Middle Income Households**
→ **Refrigerator Penetration**
→ **Total Existing Refrigerators**
→ **Annual Replacement Demand**
→ **Market Growth**
→ **New Domestic Refrigerators Sold Per Year**

---

## Assumptions

- Total population of India = **1.4 Billion**
- Urban population = **30%**
- Rural population = **70%**
- Average household size = **4 people**
- Urban household income split:
  - Upper income = **20%**
  - Middle income = **50%**
  - Lower income = **30%**
- Lower-income urban households are excluded for simplicity.
- Rural households are excluded for simplicity.
- Upper-income refrigerator penetration = **100%**
- Middle-income refrigerator penetration = **80%**
- Average refrigerator life = **10 years**
- Annual market growth = **5%**
- Second-hand refrigerators are excluded.
- Only domestic/residential refrigerators are considered.

---

## Total Population → Urban Population

| Population | Share | Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total Population | 100% | Assumption | 1.4 Billion |
| Urban Population | 30% | 1.4B × 30% | **420 Million** |
| Rural Population | 70% | 1.4B × 70% | 980 Million |

We exclude the rural population for simplicity.

**Relevant population = 420 Million**

---

## Urban Population → Urban Households

Assuming an average household size of **4 people**:

**Urban Households = 420M ÷ 4**

**Urban Households = 105 Million**

---

## Urban Households → Income Segmentation

| Income Segment | Share | Calculation | Households |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Upper Income | 20% | 105M × 20% | **21M** |
| Middle Income | 50% | 105M × 50% | **52.5M** |
| Lower Income | 30% | 105M × 30% | 31.5M |

We exclude lower-income households.

**Relevant households = 21M + 52.5M = 73.5M**

---

## Income Segmentation → Refrigerator Penetration

### Upper-Income Households

Assume **100%** of upper-income households have a refrigerator.

**21M × 100% = 21M refrigerators**

### Middle-Income Households

Assume **80%** of middle-income households have a refrigerator.

**52.5M × 80% = 42M refrigerators**

| Income Segment | Households | Refrigerator Penetration | Existing Refrigerators |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Upper Income | 21M | 100% | **21M** |
| Middle Income | 52.5M | 80% | **42M** |
| **Total** | **73.5M** | - | **63M** |

**Total existing refrigerators = 63 Million**

---

## Existing Refrigerators → Annual Replacement Demand

Assume the average life of a refrigerator is **10 years**.

Therefore, approximately 1/10th of the refrigerator base needs to be replaced every year.

**Annual Replacement Demand = 63M ÷ 10**

**Annual Replacement Demand = 6.3 Million refrigerators**

---

## Annual Replacement Demand → Market Growth

Assume the refrigerator market grows by **5% per year**.

**Annual New Refrigerator Sales = 6.3M × 105%**

**Annual New Refrigerator Sales = 6.615 Million**

≈ **6.6 Million refrigerators per year**

---

## Final Estimate

**Estimated domestic refrigerator sales in India ≈ 6.6 Million refrigerators per year**

### Rounded Guesstimate

**≈ 6–7 Million new domestic refrigerators are sold in India annually.**

---

## Summary

| Metric | Estimate |
|---|---:|
| Total Population | 1.4B |
| Urban Population | 420M |
| Urban Households | 105M |
| Upper-Income Households | 21M |
| Middle-Income Households | 52.5M |
| Existing Refrigerators | 63M |
| Annual Replacement Demand | 6.3M |
| 5% Growth Adjustment | 0.315M |
| **Final Annual Sales** | **6.615M ≈ 6.6M** |

**Final Answer: ~6.6 Million new domestic refrigerators sold in India per year.**
